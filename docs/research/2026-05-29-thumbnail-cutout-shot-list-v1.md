# Thumbnail Cutout Shot List v1

Date: 2026-05-29
Scope: Ali Abdaal / Jeff Su inspired thumbnail workflow

## Why New Shots Are Needed

The first Ali/Jeff template pass shows that the strongest reference thumbnails
use situational host poses, not only facial expressions. The reusable patterns
need cutouts that can naturally interact with UI panels, cards, icons, metrics,
and before/after scenes.

The current thumbnail generator will improve most if the cutout library covers:

- direction: left-facing, right-facing, and camera-facing variants;
- action: pointing, holding, writing, reading, thinking;
- crop: waist-up and seated desk shots;
- expression: friendly, thoughtful, surprised, serious, explaining.

## Highest Priority Shots

### 1. UI / Component Interaction

Use for Jeff-style tool panels, command panels, logo cards, and step cards.

- Pointing left, smiling/explaining, waist-up.
- Pointing right, smiling/explaining, waist-up.
- Open hands toward left-side component, friendly smile.
- Open hands toward right-side component, friendly smile.
- One hand raised as if presenting a card, looking at camera.
- Side gaze toward empty space on left, thoughtful.
- Side gaze toward empty space on right, thoughtful.

Must preserve:

- clean arm/hand silhouette;
- no fingers cut off;
- face large enough for thumbnail crop;
- leave enough body below shoulders for cropping.

### 2. Thoughtful / Decision Pose

Use for Ali social quote cards, advice thumbnails, tool comparison, and
evidence-based health claims.

- Chin touch, looking camera, slight smile.
- Chin touch, looking left.
- Chin touch, looking right.
- Serious thoughtful, looking camera.
- Slight frown/confused, looking left.
- Slight frown/confused, looking right.

Must avoid:

- exaggerated surprise unless the title truly needs it;
- hands covering mouth or jawline too much.

### 3. Desk / Study Situation

Use for Ali-style habit, productivity, research, and evidence-stack templates.

- Wearing headphones, writing in notebook, looking down-left.
- Wearing headphones, laptop open, looking down-right.
- Reading a book or paper stack, thoughtful.
- Holding a book/paper toward camera, face visible.
- Seated at desk with pen, half-body crop.
- Looking at laptop with mild smile.

These can be shot as full photos and also processed into cutouts. Keep the
desk/laptop/book visible when possible because the prop itself carries meaning.

### 4. Phone / Distraction / Before-After

Use for Ali before/after split screens and focus/dopamine content.

- Holding phone close to face, distracted/tired.
- Looking at phone with guilty or worried expression.
- Phone foreground pose, face behind it.
- Focused writing pose, clean/after version.
- Slumped/tired desk pose for before-side.
- Upright focused desk pose for after-side.

Pairing rule:

- For before/after templates, shoot matching before and after poses with similar
  camera angle and crop so they can split cleanly.

### 5. Metric / Transformation Pose

Use for Ali metric arrow and result-based thumbnails.

- Center host pointing both index fingers outward left/right.
- Center host with both palms up as if comparing two numbers.
- Excited smile, looking camera, hands low enough not to cover text.
- Confident smile, pointing to right-side metric.
- Confident smile, pointing to left-side metric.

Must preserve:

- hands should not cover the face;
- leave empty zones near both sides for numbers.

### 6. Explainer / Health Authority Pose

Use for Shosho health/longevity thumbnails where trust matters.

- Calm explaining, camera-facing.
- Warm smile, camera-facing.
- Serious but approachable, camera-facing.
- Holding supplement bottle or small object, camera-facing.
- Holding paper/research note, camera-facing.
- Doctor/teacher-like pointing to card, not salesy.

Must avoid:

- overly shocked expression for ordinary health evidence;
- medical miracle energy.

## Crop Requirements

For each high-priority pose, capture at least:

- waist-up horizontal frame;
- vertical or wider crop with full arms/hands;
- face sharp and well lit;
- no hand/finger clipped at the edge;
- neutral background or clean studio background.

## File Tagging Recommendation

Use filenames or metadata tags like:

```text
host_camera_smile_open-hands.png
host_left_thoughtful_chin-touch.png
host_right_explain_pointing-left.png
desk_headphones_writing_down-left.png
phone_before_distracted_camera.png
metric_center_two-fingers_outward.png
```

Suggested tag dimensions:

- `gaze`: camera / left / right / down-left / down-right
- `expression`: smile / thoughtful / serious / confused / surprised / explaining
- `gesture`: pointing-left / pointing-right / open-hands / chin-touch /
  writing / holding-phone / holding-book / holding-object
- `scene`: cutout / desk / phone / book / laptop / supplement
- `crop`: waist-up / torso / seated / full-arm

## Minimum Useful Batch

If time is limited, shoot these first:

1. Pointing left, friendly explaining.
2. Pointing right, friendly explaining.
3. Chin touch, looking left.
4. Chin touch, looking right.
5. Camera-facing warm smile, open hands.
6. Headphones writing at desk.
7. Laptop/reading at desk.
8. Holding phone distracted.
9. Focused writing after-state.
10. Center metric pose with two hands outward.

This minimum set covers most of the current Ali/Jeff template families.
